I welcome PhD students drawn to important questions with real-world stakes, and interested in questions of gender, social policy, and political representation. I currently supervise three doctoral researchers: [Pedro Manuel Bellón Jiménez](https://scholar.google.es/citations?user=FoTexVsAAAAJ&hl=es), [Martha Ogochukwu Dennis](https://www.linkedin.com/in/martha-ogochukwu-dennis-694ba9172/), and [Qasim Hamidi](https://webs.uab.cat/grdec/team/). If your interests overlap with these themes, I'd be glad to hear from you.

I am Director of the [Master's Programme in Political Science](https://master-ciencia-politica.uab.cat/) at UAB.
