I welcome PhD students drawn to research at the intersection of gender, social policy, and political representation. I currently supervise three doctoral researchers: [Pedro Manuel Bellón Jiménez (FPI)](https://scholar.google.es/citations?user=FoTexVsAAAAJ&hl=es), [Martha Ogochukwu Dennis (FPU)](https://www.linkedin.com/in/martha-ogochukwu-dennis-694ba9172/), and [Qasim Hamidi (FI)](https://webs.uab.cat/grdec/team/).

I am Director of the [Master's Programme in Political Science](https://master-ciencia-politica.uab.cat/) at Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona and teach courses on research methods and applied statistics at the upper undergraduate and MA levels.
